## Full description (max 4000 chars)

```
BJT Pro is your all-in-one companion for mastering Business Japanese and
preparing for the Business Japanese Proficiency Test (BJT). Whether you're
relocating to Japan, working at a Japanese company, or aiming for a top BJT
score, BJT Pro builds the real-world skills that matter in the workplace.

■ KEIGO MASTERY
Stop guessing which polite form to use. BJT Pro breaks down the three registers
of Japanese honorific language with clear, side-by-side conversions:
• Sonkeigo (尊敬語) — respectful language to elevate clients and superiors
• Kenjougo (謙譲語) — humble language for your own actions
• Teineigo (丁寧語) — the polite です/ます baseline for daily business

■ BJT MOCK TESTS
Practice with exam-style questions covering keigo, business email, telephone
manners, and vocabulary. Every question includes an instant explanation in
plain English so you learn from each answer — not just your score.

■ BUSINESS COMMUNICATION TEMPLATES
Ready-to-use Japanese for the situations you face every day:
• Polite request and apology emails
• Telephone phrases (answering, transferring, holding)
• Meeting openers and stating opinions diplomatically
Each template comes with a natural English translation.

■ CULTURAL CONTEXT
Language is only half the job. Learn the etiquette that earns trust:
• Business card exchange (名刺交換)
• Seating order — kamiza & shimoza (上座・下座)
• Hou-Ren-Sou (報連相): report, inform, consult
• After-work culture and punctuality expectations

■ WHY BJT PRO
• Clean, distraction-free design built for focused study
• Bite-sized lessons you can finish on your commute
• Authentic business Japanese, not textbook phrases
• Works offline — study anywhere

Start speaking with confidence in the Japanese workplace. Download BJT Pro and
take your first mock test today.

がんばりましょう！
```

## Required declarations checklist (Play Console)

- [ ] **Privacy policy URL** — required. The app collects no personal data, but
      Play still requires a hosted policy. Host a simple page (e.g. GitHub Pages)
      and paste the URL.
- [ ] **Data safety** — declare: no data collected, no data shared. (The app
      runs fully offline with no analytics or accounts.)
- [ ] **Content rating** — complete the questionnaire; this app rates "Everyone".
- [ ] **Target audience** — 18+/general; not directed at children.
- [ ] **Ads** — declare "No ads".
- [ ] **App access** — all functionality available without special access/login.
- [ ] **Government app** — No.
