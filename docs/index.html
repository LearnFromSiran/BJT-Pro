Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 June 2026

Summary: BJT Pro does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app runs entirely on your device, works offline, and contains no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising.

1. Information We Collect

BJT Pro does not collect any personal or sensitive user data. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

2. How the App Works

All learning content (lessons, mock tests, templates, and cultural notes) is bundled with the app and processed locally on your device. No data about your usage, answers, or progress is transmitted to us or any third party.

3. Third-Party Services

BJT Pro does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or social-media SDKs. No third party receives your information through the app.

4. Children's Privacy

BJT Pro is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any information from children. Because the app collects no data at all, no personal information from users of any age is gathered.

5. Data Security

As no data leaves your device, there is no personal data for us to secure or that could be exposed in a server-side breach.

6. Permissions

The app requests only the standard permissions required to run (such as internet access for system functions). It does not use device permissions to collect personal information.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
